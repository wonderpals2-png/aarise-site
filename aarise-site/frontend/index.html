Built for the moment it matters

Protection that doesn't wait for a good connection.

Every feature answers the same question: what happens in the ten seconds when someone can't reach for their phone, type a message, or explain where they are?

Tiered SOS, one button

A tap, a hold, and a long-hold each trigger a different response — from a silent location ping to contacts, to a loud local alarm. No menus to navigate under stress.

Offline-first, over cellular

Alerts go out over LTE via an onboard SIM, not Wi-Fi or Bluetooth-to-phone. If the wearer's phone is dead, left behind, or out of range, Aarise still reaches out on its own.

Live location, shared automatically

GPS fix is attached to every alert and updates as the wearer moves, so the people who respond are following a live trail, not a last-known pin from an hour ago.

"Get Me Home"

One deep link books a ride from wherever the wearer is, shares the trip with their contacts in real time, and keeps the SOS tier active until they're signed in safe.

Motion-aware alerting

An onboard motion sensor recognises sudden falls or a struggle, and can raise a tier on its own — the wearer doesn't always have to be the one who presses anything.

A quiet trusted circle

Contacts are set once, ranked by priority, and notified in order — so an alert always reaches someone, even if the first person doesn't pick up.

How a single press escalates

Three tiers. One motion to learn, once.

The same button means something different depending on how it's held — built so it still works when hands are shaking.

01

Tap

Drops a silent location pin to your trusted circle. No sound, no screen change — for when you just want someone watching.

02

Hold — 3s

Sends a full alert with live location over cellular, and opens a call window to your first responder.

03

Hold — 6s

Triggers a local audible alarm and escalates to every contact in your circle, in priority order, until one responds.

Available at any tier — books a ride, shares the trip live, and keeps your circle updated until you're home.

What's inside Aarise One

Simple hardware, chosen for reliability over range.

MicrocontrollerESP32 — handles sensor input, button tiers, and local logic on the wrist.
Cellular moduleSIM7000E (LTE / 4G) — sends alerts independent of the wearer's phone or Wi-Fi.
PositioningNEO-M8N GPS — attaches a live location fix to every alert and ride request.
Motion sensingMPU6050 accelerometer + gyroscope — recognises falls and sudden motion to raise a tier automatically.
Form factorWatch-worn, single physical button, designed to be operated without looking at it.
